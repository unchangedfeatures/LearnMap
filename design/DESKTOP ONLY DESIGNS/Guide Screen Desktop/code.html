Understanding the Self-Attention Mechanism

The core innovation of the Transformer model is the attention mechanism. Unlike recurrent layers, attention allows the model to "look" at all parts of the input sequence simultaneously, regardless of their distance from the current token.

Figure 1.1: Schematic representation of the Scaling Dot-Product Attention.

1. The Intuition

Imagine you are reading a sentence. When you encounter the word 'it', your brain subconsciously looks back at previous words to resolve what 'it' refers to. Self-attention does this mathematically by assigning weights to all other tokens based on their relevance.

lightbulb Key Insight

Attention is not about looking at everything; it's about knowing what to ignore.

2. Mathematical Formulation

We define three learnable weight matrices: Query (Q), Key (K), and Value (V). The output is calculated as:

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √dₖ)V
